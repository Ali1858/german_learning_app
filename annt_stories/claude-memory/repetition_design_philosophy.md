The reader's core pedagogical design (stated by the user at the very start of the project) is that words get internalized through repetition **across** stories — introduced in one story, then naturally reused in later stories as "old vocabulary" in different phrases/cases — not by stuffing many repetitions of the same word into its single introducing story.

**Why:** When a content-completeness gap was found (40 target nouns with too few annotated occurrences) and initially fixed by adding a 2nd occurrence within the SAME home story, the user clarified: the baseline pattern where most of the 500 target nouns get only ONE annotated occurrence in their home story, with reuse coming from later stories, is "perfectly fine, we need that only." A later "increase to 4 occurrences" request meant 4 occurrences spread across the course (2 in the home story + 2 more woven naturally into later stories), not 4 crammed into one place.

**How to apply:** When adding or expanding vocabulary coverage, prefer weaving a word into a later, unrelated-but-plausible story scene (a phone call, a memory, a reflective aside) over repeating it multiple times within its own introduction story. See `workflow_edit_framework_not_html.md` for how such edits should be made, and `german_reader_project_structure.md` for where the content lives.
